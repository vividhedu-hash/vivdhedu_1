Study-abroad agents earn ₹2L–₹4L per student placed — paid by the foreign college, not by you. They push you toward whatever college pays them the most, even if the loan will cripple you financially.
Salary numbers come from college brochures — not real data. Nobody tells you which jobs AI will automate in 5 years. Nobody checks if your loan is actually payable on your expected salary.
